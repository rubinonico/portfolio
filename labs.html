Multi-sensor fusion system for real-time cut-point identification in batch distillation. Replaces subjective human judgment with objective data.

Morgul / Silmaril — USB Passkey Generator

Hardware passkey generator. Plugs in, authenticates. Unplugs, locks. Deterministic HMAC password generation from a seed — no stored secrets on the host.

  • USB device, no driver needed
  • Auto-login on plug, auto-lock on unplug
  • Seed-based, no cloud dependency
