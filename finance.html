Multi-Strategy Systematic Trading Framework

01

A modular systematic crypto trading architecture that fuses three independent signal families into a single portfolio allocation layer. Graph Signal Processing extracts structure from cross-asset correlation networks, a Temporal Convolutional Network captures momentum dynamics, and a Hidden Markov Model segments the market into latent regimes to gate strategy exposure. Signals are blended and risk-weighted before execution, with the entire pipeline evaluated under strict walk-forward validation to avoid lookahead bias.

Python NumPy PyTorch scikit-learn hmmlearn NetworkX pandas
Key results
  • 2.49 Sharpe on walk-forward out-of-sample evaluation
  • Regime detection via HMM dynamically reduces exposure in adverse states
  • Graph Signal Processing surfaces cross-asset lead-lag structure for signals
  • TCN momentum module outperforms baseline momentum on rolling re-fits

DeFi Liquidity Provision Research

02

A systematic investigation of concentrated liquidity provision on Uniswap V3. The study models impermanent loss and fee accrual under realistic market conditions, using Heterogeneous AutoRegressive (HAR) models for realized volatility forecasting and copula models to capture the joint price dynamics of paired assets. The work culminates in a published negative result: at retail scale, concentrated LP returns fail to compensate for impermanent loss and gas costs relative to simply holding the underlying assets.

Python pandas NumPy SciPy statsmodels web3.py matplotlib
Key results
  • Published negative result — retail-scale LP underperforms buy-and-hold after fees & IL
  • HAR volatility forecasts quantify optimal liquidity range widths
  • Copula models reveal tail dependence that amplifies impermanent loss in drawdowns
  • On-chain fee & position data pulled directly via web3.py for empirical grounding

LP Engine

03

An on-chain liquidity engine built around BTC-collateralized borrowing. The system borrows against BTC collateral to fund a delta-neutral WETH/USDC concentrated liquidity position, with the directional risk of the LP neutralized through perpetual futures hedges. Position sizing is driven by a ratio-volatility model that scales exposure to the realized volatility spread between the LP's fee yield and the cost of the hedge, keeping the book delta-flat while harvesting fee income.

Python Solidity web3.py NumPy pandas ccxt
Key results
  • Delta-neutral WETH/USDC LP position with perpetual hedge overlay
  • BTC-collateralized borrowing keeps capital efficiency high without selling the base asset
  • Ratio-volatility sizing adapts exposure to the fee-yield vs. hedge-cost spread
  • Automated rebalancing & hedge roll logic under continuous position monitoring
